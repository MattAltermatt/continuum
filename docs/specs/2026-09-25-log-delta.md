# The log's delta: how much sooner than last life

*2026-09-25. Issue #75. Brainstormed with the user the same day; the look is
variant E of the mockup `docs/mockups/2026-09-25-log-delta.html` (and its
`.png`), the user's pick. Every player-facing choice below is the user's,
picked on that mockup or stated outright.*

**Status:** built on `feature/log-delta` as 0.3.3; plan `docs/plans/2026-09-25-log-delta.md`. Amended during the build: storage is one `lastFinish` map, not a per-life record (plan Revision 1); both maps keep only one-time rows at ticks of at least 0; the delta cell carries hidden words for a screen reader.

## 1. What the user asked for

> for the log, add in the amount of time it took to finish the single actions,
> and show the delta. For example, 'Rig the hull' finished at 2:15 (for
> example) last time it was 2:30, show this delta.

And, on the first round of the mockup: *the time and the delta should be
close.*

**Found at design time:** every log line already starts with the run clock
(`src/ui/Log.tsx`, `log__at`), so "the hull finished at 2:15" is on screen
today. The work is the comparison alone.

Read literally, and not reopened:

- **Single actions** are one-time rows (`isOneTime`): the hull, the net, the
  satchel, the pirates, the sails. A repeatable row's completion is not news
  and is not logged; that stays.
- **Last time** is the last life that finished the same row, not the best one.
  A life that died before reaching the row is skipped.
- **The first finish ever** has nothing to compare with: its delta is blank.

## 2. The look (variant E)

The log line gets a second fixed column between the time and the text:

```text
02:15  −0:15  The hull holds. Let the wind try harder.
02:31  +0:40  A trawl net, fine as smoke. The shallows give up more.
03:05  ±0:00  A canvas satchel. Room for more of everything.
02:50         Out of scrap
03:40         The harbor pirates scatter. The dock is ours again.
```

- **Sooner** is `−m:ss` in `--good`; **later** is `+m:ss` in `--hurt-text`;
  **the same** is `±0:00` in `--ink-3`. The minus is U+2212 and the plus-minus
  U+00B1, written as escapes in `src/ui/glyphs.ts` (the house rule for spec
  glyphs; `MINUS` is already there).
- The column is on **every** line, blank where there is no delta, so every
  sentence starts at one edge. Its width is pinned to fit `+999:59`, and the
  time column's to fit `100:00` (measured: 46.4px and 39.7px at 11px Menlo;
  the columns are 48px and 42px, leaving room for a fallback monospace face),
  so a line never moves when a delta arrives and a life past 100 minutes does
  not clip. The time and delta columns are sized in a
  unit that does not change between a story line (12px serif) and a note line
  (11px mono), so the text edge is one edge on both; the mockup's first cut
  sized them in `em` and the two kinds of line did not line up.
- The delta is set in the mono face, upright, at 11px, on story lines too.
- **The delta is the difference of the two printed clocks**, not of the raw
  ticks: `floor(at seconds) − floor(lastAt seconds)`. The player can do the
  subtraction from what they see and get the same answer, and a difference
  under a second reads `±0:00`, never `−0:00`.
- Minutes are not zero-padded in the delta (`0:15`, `12:30`); the time column
  keeps its `mm:ss`.

## 3. What is remembered

**This life's split times:** `GameState.finishedAt: Record<ActionId, number>`,
the run tick at which each one-time row completed in this life. `complete()`
in `src/engine/queue.ts` stamps it with `s.runTicks` where it already appends
to `completedOneTime`. `step` (`src/engine/tick.ts`) raises `runTicks` before
it works the row, so the stamp is the same number the log writes as the line's
`at` (`useGame.ts` `withLog`). `blankRun` starts it empty: it resets with the
life.

**The last time each row was finished:** `GameState.lastFinish:
Record<ActionId, number>`, one entry per one-time row ever finished, holding
the tick of the most recent life that finished it. `rebirth` merges the dying
life into it, `{ ...dead.lastFinish, ...dead.finishedAt }`, so a life that
never reached a row leaves that row's older time standing (life 5 at 2:30,
life 6 short, life 7 compares with 2:30). It persists across lives and is
derived at rebirth, like `lives`.

**The comparison travels on the event.** The `completed` event gains an
optional `lastAt?: number`, set to `s.lastFinish[id]` when the row is one-time
and the map has it, and absent otherwise (no `lastAt: undefined` key). The log
line keeps the event, so a line from life 6 still shows life 6's comparison
while life 7 plays: computing the delta in the UI from the current state would
compare an old line against the wrong life.

**Log lines are saved with their events,** so a line written before this
change has no `lastAt` and shows a blank delta: what the line said when it was
written.

**Known gap, accepted:** a save from before this change has no `lastFinish`
and a life already under way has no `finishedAt` for the rows it finished
before the load. The next life therefore shows a **blank** delta for those
rows (as for a first finish), and every life after that compares normally.
Only a state built by hand through the dev handle, with a `lastFinish` that
holds a row its `finishedAt` lacks, can compare with an older life than the
last.

## 4. Saves

The save format number does **not** change: every new field is additive and a
save from before it loads.

- `reconcile` in `src/state/save.ts` gives a state without `finishedAt` or
  `lastFinish` an empty map, keeps only rows the book has (`keep(..., row)`, as
  for `completionCounts`), and drops any value that is not a finite number.
- `isLine` checks that `lastAt`, when present, is a finite number, so a
  hand-edited line cannot render `NaN:NaN`; a line that fails reads as corrupt,
  as a malformed line does today.
- The reducer's `load` of a hand-built state (the dev handle) gives a state
  without `finishedAt` or `lastFinish` empty maps, beside its `lives` default,
  so the engine never guards either.
- `LifeRecord` is unchanged.

## 5. What does not change

- No balance number: there is no tuning in this change.
- The death overlay, its chart and `LifeRecord` are untouched.
- Which events reach the log (`news` in `src/state/useGame.ts`) is unchanged.

## 6. Tests

- **Engine:** a one-time completion stamps `finishedAt` with the `runTicks` of
  the step that emitted `completed`; a repeatable one does not. `rebirth`
  resets `finishedAt` and merges it into `lastFinish`, keeping an older entry
  for a row the life did not reach. `lastAt` is the last life that finished
  the row (life 5 at 2:30, life 6 short, life 7 compares with 2:30; a newer,
  slower life replaces a faster older one); absent on a first finish and on
  repeatable rows. With the same inputs in two lives, the second is sooner
  (core levels carry). The property test checks after every action and tick
  that every key of `finishedAt` is in `completedOneTime` with a value at most
  `runTicks`, and every key of `lastFinish` is a one-time row of the book with
  a finite value at least 0.
- **Save:** states without either map load with empty ones; a row the book no
  longer has is dropped; a non-finite value is dropped; a round-trip keeps the
  values; a line whose `lastAt` is not a finite number reads as corrupt.
- **Log component:** sooner, later and the same render the right sign, glyph
  and class; a sub-second difference whose clocks agree reads `±0:00`; no
  `lastAt` renders an empty delta cell; every line has the cell.
- **Chrome:** at 390 and 1280, a sooner finish shows a green `−` and a later
  one a red `+` beside the time; note lines and story lines share one
  text edge; `100:00` and `+999:59` fit their cells.
