## Accepted risks

Each is the objection, the cheaper alternative, and why it was rejected.
- *Task 1 migrates about 20 files.* Alternative: keep `Chapter.order`/`event`
  with optional `pages`. Rejected: two book shapes and two validator paths
  forever, and an ambiguous format for the generator (#52, #53); the migration
  is mechanical and one-time.
- *The pirates lose today's food stock-up.* Alternative: a fill before every
  closer, reset per page. Rejected: provisioning is "before travel" and a page
  turn is not travel; a fill per page costs time on every page; the fight
  back-off (#74) protects the pirates. Task 6 reads both ways.
- *A closer's automation chip orders it only once its next prerequisite can
  start* (revised at 2a: the earlier "does nothing until its page is done"
  froze a prioritized run, since a JIT one-time has no chip-driven demand of
  its own). Alternative: order a waiting closer whenever its chip is on.
  Rejected: when the pull fails, the chip re-orders it every tick without
  time passing.

## Risks

- Task 2 changes what every press does (spec 4.3), so it restates more
  existing tests than any task so far. Each restatement is named in its
  commit body for the code panel to read.
- The whole-book bands are skipped from the task they turn red until task 8.
  A band moved without the user is a tuning change.
- A cycle between two one-times of a page (each spending the other's output)
  is not caught by the validator; it goes with #53's generator.
