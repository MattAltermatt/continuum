The death card: what the life bought, then a button

Shown when health reaches 0. The reset has already happened behind it: the pack and queue are empty, run levels are back to 0, and the health bar is full at the new maximum. The card lists only gains. Skills that did not move are left off. Nothing ticks until the player presses Begin, and after that nothing ticks until something is queued (decision #41).
