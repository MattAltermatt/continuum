The big sink: what A and B look like in play

Every number here comes from running the v0.1 plan's own engine headless (Revision 7), not from a sketch. The hall's numbers (500 stone, 5000 XP) are illustrative placeholders, not a proposal to tune.

What the log reads under B (hall + Mine, first minute, from the engine)

    The hall and Mine take turns: each stone that lands lets the hall go on, and the hall waits again once it has spent it. That is two log lines per stone, so B also needs the log capped (a Task 6 change). Under A the log goes quiet within a few minutes, because nothing is happening.
