Everything else is the death card: the clock, core gains with bars (skills that did not move are left off), max health from → to with its arithmetic.
  • Read again dispatches the same begin as a death: the next life starts at I · Port Cinder with core ledgers, automation and completion counts kept (BOYS 8), and the life's max-health gain applied as a death's would be (spec 9). It takes focus when the card opens, as Begin does.
