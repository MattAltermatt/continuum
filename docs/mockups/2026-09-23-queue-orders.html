The queue of orders: the row, the refusal, the queue

reading it
  1. The working top. Only the top runs (2.1): the ember border, the sheen, the 12px ember bar with this completion's value under it, and the countdown at the current rate. It is a JIT supply: automation put it at index 0 when the hull reached the top short, so it carries auto. The look-ahead (2.3) sums what the entries below still need of scrap, down to the next scrap maker: the hull owes the last 3 of its 8, so this order stops after 3, under the cap of 5, and says so.
  2. Kept progress. The hull's work lives on the row (2.2): 37.5/60.0 xp and 5/8 scrap spent on an earlier try survive the pop and are never paid twice. A one-time is always once. Passive fill queued it on high when the queue ran dry (auto). Below the top a bar is grey: nothing below the top moves. ⚠︎ marks what the pack cannot pay right now.
  3. A repeat order (a plain click on +). A repeating producer with nothing below it that needs fish fills to the cap, then pops.
  4. A once order: a one-time row. Its hurt shows on the order as on the row.
  5. A once order of a repeatable (Shift+click on +): exactly one completion, to top the galley up after the fight.
  • × removes on one press: removing loses nothing, because the work stays on the row. The armed “?” is gone (2.2).
  • Every entry keeps four lines, the last always present, so no entry changes height and no × moves under the cursor.
  • repeat/once share one pinned width, so a tag never shifts the row; auto is in the automation color.
  • No total in the header: a repeating order's length depends on what it fetches (09-22 8.1: not accurate, not shown).
