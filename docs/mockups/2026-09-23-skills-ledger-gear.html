where each number comes from
  • core: level 40 costs 10 × 1.1^40 = 452.6 xp; 187.3 of it is done. At 4.05 xp/s the rest takes 66 s, shown as a countdown because Fish is running. Factor 1 + 40 × 5% = ×3.00.
  • run: level 8 costs 25 × 1.1^8 = 53.6 xp. Factor 1 + 8 × 1% = ×1.08. best ever is a new per-skill counter kept across lives (spec 8).
  • gear: the trawl net's effect, made this life, so it resets at death with the rest of the life (spec 6.3). Each piece by its row's noun with its factor; with none it reads none and ×1.00. With two pieces the factor column shows their product over the factors, e.g. ×1.50 over 1.25 × 1.20.
  • total: 3.00 × 1.08 × 1.25 = ×4.05, the same string as the cell. At 0.1 xp a tick and 10 ticks a second, that is 4.05 xp/s, and a 4 xp fish takes 1.0s.
  • lifetime: xp is derived, every core level's cost below 40 plus the 187.3 in hand (4,425.9 + 187.3). done sums the completions of Fish's rows (1,033 at the cloud shallows, 60 at the eel runs). time is the new ticks counter.
