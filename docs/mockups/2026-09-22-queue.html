Rules in this mock: (1) fractions are always a/b, no spaces. (2) Every entry shows the time its remaining work takes at the current rate; the header and footer sum them. Waiting time is not in the sum, hence "plus whatever the ⚠ cost". (3) ⚠ beside an input means "right now the pack does not hold what this still needs", on any entry that is not running; the running entry says it in words instead. (4) The fix button appears only when nothing ahead in the queue makes the missing thing. The boat has no button because Chop is already in front of it; the smokehouse has one for stone because nothing queued makes stone. The warn hue is a straw yellow, distinct from ember and from hurt.
