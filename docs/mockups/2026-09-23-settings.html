Settings behind the gear: one entry, and a dev-only speed

The gear becomes a button. It opens a small panel under itself, right-aligned, over the clock and the end of the skills band. For now the panel has one entry, erase save, which takes two presses. Below a rule, a build served by the dev server adds a segmented speed control; a production build never has it.

What a player sees: the one entry. No rule, no speed, no trace of the dev control.

  • Opening and closing: the gear opens and closes the panel on click or Enter; Escape and a click outside close it. The panel's width is fixed, so “erase save” and “press again to erase” swap in the same box and nothing moves under the cursor.
  • Speed multiplies the ticks run per real interval: ×10 and ×100, to reach an end while testing (spec 10). It exists only under import.meta.env.DEV. The dev handle gains the same speed, save and erase.
  • Every control here is at least 24px tall with the active edge; the armed state is the only hurt on the panel.
