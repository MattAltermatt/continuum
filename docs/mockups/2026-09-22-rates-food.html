Left: the new chunk. Everything that moves the health bar per second, in one column, with the net at the bottom. Right: food redone. No "worth", no "/ 5s". Each food shows what one bite heals and a cooldown bar that drains until that food can be eaten again; the bite happens when the bar empties.

Every line in the rates chunk is a rate that is true this second: decay from the curve, the running action's drain if it has one, and the larder's ceiling from its cooldowns. Net is their sum. It is red when hp is falling and green when the larder can more than cover it; it is not a prediction of anything. The health chunk keeps only the bar and 71 / 100; its decay line moves here. In the food chunk the cooldown bars are the "next bite" for each food, so no number needs dividing.
